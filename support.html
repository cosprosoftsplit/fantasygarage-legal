Frequently asked questions

I bought the Collector's Edition on another iPhone — how do I get it back?
Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases" (it is also on the Collector's Edition sheet). Purchases made with the same Apple ID are restored, and the four collector bikes return to your garage.
What exactly is in the Collector's Edition?
Four collector bikes delivered to your garage as restoration projects, eight premium finishes, two showroom environments and two showroom layouts. It is a one-time purchase. There are no ads and no subscriptions.
Are these real motorcycles?
No. Every marque and model in Fantasy Garage is invented for the game. No real motorcycle brands, models or badges appear.
Where are my My Bikes photos stored?
Only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage, with location and camera metadata removed. Nothing is uploaded. Delete a bike from My Bikes, reset the game in Settings, or remove the app to delete them.
I deleted the app. Can I get my garage back?
Your garage lives on your device and in your own device backups. Restore Purchases restores the Collector's Edition, not a deleted garage.
The app is not working correctly. What should I do?
Close and reopen the app, and make sure you have the latest version installed. If the problem persists, email us a short description of what you did and what happened.
