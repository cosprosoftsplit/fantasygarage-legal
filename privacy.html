Fantasy Garage: Moto — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

In short: this app collects no personal data. It has no server, no user account, no sign-in, no analytics and no ads. Everything you add stays in the app's data area on your iPhone.

Data Controller

Cosmic Production d.o.o.
Split, Croatia
Email: ivan@cosmicproduction.hr

What the app handles, and where it stays

Your garage (the bikes you collect, their restorations, builds, paint and showroom) is saved in the app's protected data area on your device. The app has no server component and contains no code that sends your game or your entries anywhere. The provider has no access to them at any time.

My Bikes and photos. If you add your own motorcycles to the My Bikes shelf, the photo you choose with the system photo picker is copied into the app's own storage on your device. Photo metadata such as location (GPS) and camera details is removed when the photo is imported. The app does not access your photo library beyond the photo you pick. The make, model, year, mileage, story and service date you type are stored on your device only. Nothing is uploaded.

Notifications (optional). If you turn on a reminder (a finished Workshop job or a next-service date), the app asks for permission and schedules local notifications on your device. They are created on the device; no notification data is sent to us or to any server.

Sharing. If you share a showroom card, you choose where it goes using the system share sheet. The provider does not receive it.

Device backups. Your garage may be included in your own device backup (iCloud or computer backup) under your settings with Apple; the provider has no access to it.

No analytics, no tracking, no ads

The app integrates no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, attribution or tracking components and uses no third-party SDK. It builds no usage profile, reads no device or advertising identifier, and never shows an App Tracking Transparency request.

Purchases

The app offers one optional one-time in-app purchase, the Collector's Edition. The purchase and the payment are handled entirely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). The provider receives no payment details, no card data and no account identifier — the app only checks whether a valid purchase exists.

Legal basis and retention

Because the provider neither collects nor processes personal data, no processing by the provider takes place within the meaning of the GDPR. Your data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, reset the game in Settings or remove the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Because the provider holds no data about you, there is nothing it can disclose or erase on request — control over your data rests directly with you, on your device.

Platform

Fantasy Garage: Moto is available for iPhone (iOS) only.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 16, and the provider does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's functionality changes in a way that matters for data protection, this policy is updated before that version is released.
