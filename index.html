Fantasy Garage: Moto

A motorcycle collection and restoration simulation game for iPhone. Collect, restore, build and paint original fictional motorcycles, line them up in your showroom, and park the bikes you ride on a private My Bikes shelf. Every marque and model in the game is invented; no real motorcycle brands, models or badges appear.
